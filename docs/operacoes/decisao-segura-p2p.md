# P2P safety: a quick decision checklist

Use this checklist before sending money or releasing cryptoassets. It is educational guidance, not a guarantee of safety.

## Before the trade
1. Check the counterparty and the platform's official trade record.
2. Confirm the asset, network, amount, recipient and applicable payment method.
3. Reject requests to continue privately or change recipients outside the documented process.

## If you are paying
Verify the recipient against the trade instructions. A screenshot or chat message is not independent confirmation of the receiving account.

## If you are selling
Verify receipt using your own bank or payment-provider records. A receipt image, notification or claim of transfer is not final settlement.

## If something does not match
Pause, preserve the transaction identifiers and contact the platform's documented dispute channel. Do not release funds to resolve pressure or a threat of a poor rating.

## For merchants
Separate the person who reconciles incoming payments from the person who authorizes release when your workflow supports it. Keep a minimal audit trail and protect customer data.

## Limits
Bank transfers can have different reversal rules depending on jurisdiction and payment method. Escrow cannot independently prove fiat payment finality.

**Claim classification:** General guidance; not a statement of implemented Sails Protocol controls.
