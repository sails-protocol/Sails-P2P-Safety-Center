# Arbitration safety: what an arbitrator can and cannot guarantee

An arbitrator helps assess a dispute; they do not automatically verify bank settlement, reverse a transfer, or guarantee that funds will be recovered.

## Before accepting arbitration
- Confirm the dispute channel and identity through the platform's official interface, not an unsolicited direct message.
- Understand which evidence you are authorized to view and which decision powers actually exist.
- Never promise a guaranteed outcome or request payment to an unrelated account.

## During a dispute
- Preserve original transaction records and timestamps; redact unrelated personal information.
- Separate independently verifiable facts from screenshots, claims and interpretations.
- Disclose conflicts of interest and avoid private arrangements with either party.

## If an arbitrator acts in bad faith
Record the event through the platform's documented escalation path. Do not assume an arbitrator's bond, election, slashing or withdrawal process is live unless the deployed protocol explicitly documents and proves it.

## Limits and privacy
This is general educational guidance, not an operational specification for Sails Protocol arbitration. Escrow control over cryptoassets does not establish fiat finality or the authenticity of payment evidence.

**Claim classification:** General guidance. No implemented arbitrator selection, collateral or penalty mechanism is asserted.
