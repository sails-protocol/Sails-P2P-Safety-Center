# Unexpected payment recipient

**Scenario:** your bank, payment provider, or wallet shows a recipient or destination different from the payment method and details previously registered or agreed for the trade.

**Before sending:** do not confirm impulsively. Compare the original instructions, check for changes, and pause the payment if you cannot resolve the mismatch through a trusted channel.

**If already sent:** keep the transaction record, record the time, promptly contact the relevant bank, payment provider, or wallet service about available procedures, and follow the trade's support or dispute process.

Do not rely solely on screenshots, QR codes, or messages outside the agreed flow.

**Status:** general guidance. Crypto escrow does not automatically reverse payments made through external payment methods.
